Before you enquire

Three things procurement asks

Can our organization set up a corporate account with billing?

Yes. Organizations booking regularly can arrange a corporate account so trips are billed together rather than paid per ride. Contact us with your expected volume and billing contact and we will confirm the arrangement in writing before the first trip.

Can you handle multiple vehicles for a conference or group?

Yes. Multi-vehicle movements are coordinated in advance rather than dispatched on the day. Send the delegate numbers, venues and timings and we will confirm what we can commit to before you build it into your schedule.

Do you cover business travel outside Charlottetown?

Yes. Corporate work runs across Prince Edward Island, including Stratford, Cornwall, Summerside, the Confederation Bridge and the ferry terminals, with the price agreed before departure.
